How to Tell You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water has reached the electrical panel, appliances or power strips on the floor.
  • Pooled water is creeping over the floor into neighboring rooms or onto the staircase.
  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
  • Nobody was home when it started, so the length of the leak is unknown.
  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
  • Wet carpet, wood flooring or wallboard has stayed saturated longer than a few hours.
  • Water is still flowing and you cannot find or reach the shutoff valve.

Maintenance Tips for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Before anything goes wrong, store the phone number for an emergency restoration crew.
  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.
  • Find your main water shutoff today and make sure everyone in the house can close it.
  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season and consider a battery backup.
  • Swap worn hoses feeding the washer, sinks and toilets rather than waiting for a rupture.
  • Being able to find the electrical panel quickly means wet areas can be de-energized without risk.

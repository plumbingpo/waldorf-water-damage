Water Damage Prevention for Businesses in Waldorf, MD

Home / Blog / Commercial Water Damage Prevention in Waldorf, MD

For owners and managers of commercial property in Waldorf, MD, few risks cost as much as water damage. Restoration is only the direct cost, because a commercial loss also brings potential liability exposure, displaced employees, lost inventory, and interrupted business. Preventing damage ahead of time costs far less than restoring after the fact. Below are the most effective ways to keep water damage away from a commercial property in Waldorf.

A Maintenance Program for Your Building

A comprehensive building maintenance program is the foundation of water damage prevention. On a regular basis, have someone check the roof, the plumbing, HVAC units, the fire suppression system, and the integrity of the building envelope. Keep written records of every repair and inspection, since they show due diligence, which can limit your liability and back up an insurance claim after a loss.

Water Detection Systems

A commercial water detection system warns you about a leak early, before the damage becomes significant. Today's equipment is able to monitor supply lines, water heaters, places that tend to flood, and HVAC condensate lines. A few models will even cut the water supply on their own once they sense a leak. Preventing even a single significant water event typically repays the cost of detection technology many times over.

Preparing for a Water Emergency

A written water emergency plan belongs in every commercial building, and it should spell out who is trained to shut off the main water and how, what each employee does during a water event, how tenants and stakeholders will be told, how inventory and critical documents get protected, and whom to call, with numbers for property management, your insurance agent, and Waldorf Restoration at (970) 225-5415 for restoration.

What to Review in Your Insurance

Once a year, sit down with your agent and go over the commercial property policy. Ensure your coverage includes business interruption insurance, adequate building and contents coverage, and endorsements for equipment breakdown and backup of sewers and drains. Comprehensive coverage raises premiums by a relatively modest amount, so compare that with the cost of being underinsured.

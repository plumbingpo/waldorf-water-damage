Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Across Waldorf, Waldorf Restoration answers sewage emergencies 24/7, sending trained technicians who arrive with EPA-registered disinfectants and the right protective equipment so your property is decontaminated safely.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Never pour grease, oil or food waste down your drains, since these cause blockages
  • Fit a strainer over each sink and shower drain so hair and debris stay out of the pipes
  • A backwater valve at the sewer connection stops the city system from backing up into your home
  • Every 2 to 3 years, get a sewer camera run through the line to spot developing issues
  • Flush nothing but toilet paper, because wipes, even 'flushable' ones, cause clogs
  • Roots from trees close to the sewer line work their way into pipes and block them, so have them cleared

How to Tell You Need Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Several fixtures backing up at once instead of only one
  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix
  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • A rotten egg or sewer smell from the drains or the lowest floor of the home
  • Unexplained illness or gastrointestinal symptoms among household members
  • Carpet or flooring contaminated when a toilet overflowed with solid waste

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. Because raw sewage is a biological hazard, the work demands trained people, protective equipment (gloves, boots, respirators and Tyvek suits) and disinfection products registered with the EPA and not sold to the public. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.
